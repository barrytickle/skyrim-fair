# The Wanderer's Fair

## Description

A travelling festival has pitched its walls beside the road west of Whiterun, and everyone's
invited.

The Wanderer's Fair is a walled festival ground of its own, with a stage show, a market of 33
stalls, an archery range, fireworks, and a crowd that dances, claps and cheers along with the
music. Follow the road sign, go through the gate, and spend an evening somewhere Skyrim
isn't trying to kill you.

It's meant to feel like a real place that happens to be having a very good week. It isn't a
parody. The humour comes from the fair itself: the suspiciously successful sweetroll stall,
the Fair Inspector's increasingly desperate notices, and the horse on the stage roof that
nobody can explain.

## Installation instructions

1. Install the requirements below. Holidays is a master, so the fair won't load without it.
2. Install the main file with **Mod Organizer 2** or **Vortex** ("Mod Manager Download").
3. Enable `SkyrimFair.esp`. It loads anywhere after `Holidays.esp`.
4. **If you use a grass cache** (No Grass In Objects / Grass Cache Helper NG, as most big
   modlists do), also install the **Grass Cache** optional file. Without it, the fair's ground
   has no grass. If you generate your own grass cache, do it with the fair installed and it
   will be included.
5. **If you use Maximum Destruction, Stealth Detection Fixes or Strange Runes,** make the
   one-word edits in the Compatibility section below. **Without them, Maximum Destruction and
   Stealth Detection Fixes freeze the game at the fair.**
6. **To find the fair:** it stands beside the road west of Whiterun, towards the Western
   Watchtower. Look for the road sign, the banners and the braziers at its gate.

**Updating:** a save keeps some of the stage show's data. If an update's notes say it changes
the show, start a new game or load a save from before you first entered the fair.

**Uninstalling:** as with any mod that adds places, scripts and people, don't remove it from a
save you intend to keep playing.

## Main features

**A festival ground of its own**
- A walled compound with its own worldspace: a palisade and gate, stalls in lanes and a
  market cross-street, light towers, a green, and animal pens.
- An entrance in Tamriel, by the road: the gate, braziers, banners and lamp posts. You can
  hear the music faintly from outside, and at night there are fireworks over the walls.
- Your companions come in and out through the gate with you.

**The stage show**
- Six original songs, played in turn, starting with "The Wanderer's Fair".
- A band and an orchestra playing along, and three singers at the front of the stage.
- The crowd dances, claps and cheers along, and there are fireworks after every song.
- A pair of folk dancers on the green, with their own paired dance.
- Optional: with **Professional Dancer** installed, the crowd's dancers use five of its
  dances (detected automatically).

**The market**
- 33 stalls, each with its own trade and its own goods on the counter: bakers, brewers, a
  cheesemonger, a fishmonger, smiths, Imperial and Stormcloak armourers, elven and Dwemer
  goods, a fletcher, a mage's stall, a herbalist, a jeweller, a toy stall, a fortune teller,
  and more.
- Every keeper runs a real shop, stocked for their trade and restocked the vanilla way. Talk
  to them, or look at the counter and choose **Browse**.
- **Fair prices:** the armour and gear stalls charge 20 times the usual price, so the fair is
  a treat, not a shortcut to the best gear. Everything else sells at normal prices, and
  selling to the keepers is unchanged. Outside the fair, prices are untouched.

**Life at the fair**
- A varied crowd of visitors, including children and people sat taking it all in.
- Archers practising at the range.
- Goats, chickens and dogs, and a horse in the pen.
- Two travellers you might meet more than once: **Garrick Sol V** and **Claudius Vale**,
  both fully voiced.
- The Fair Inspector's notices on the palisade, regarding an ongoing investigation.
- A horse on the stage roof. Nobody knows how it got up there.

**Built to run well**
- Visitors you can't see are switched off as you move around the fair, which keeps the frame
  rate up despite the crowd.
- The fair's own scripts use vanilla Papyrus only.

## Requirements

**Required**
- Skyrim Special Edition or Anniversary Edition
- **[Holidays](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/1533)**: the fair's
  festival rope lines, lanterns and some props are its records.

**Recommended**
- **Open Animation Replacer** (needs **SKSE**): the folk dance, the band's fast and held
  instrument loops, and the singers' own cheer. Without it, they fall back to vanilla
  animations.
- **[Professional Dancer](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/124608)**
  (optional): enable `Dance.esp`, then run Pandora or Nemesis. Nothing of it is included.
- **[Terrain Parallax](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/54860)**: the
  ground's parallax. A visual replacer only.

**Compatibility:** mods that give every NPC in the game a spell can overload the fair's crowd.
For Maximum Destruction, Stealth Detection Fixes and Strange Runes, the fix is a one-word
edit to each mod's own file: see the Compatibility section.
