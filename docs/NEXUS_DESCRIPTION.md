# The Wanderer's Fair

## Description

**Skyrim has dragons, draugr and a civil war. What it doesn't have is somewhere to just enjoy
an evening out. So I built it one.**

I wanted somewhere in Skyrim that felt... alive. Somewhere the people who live there do
something other than wander aimlessly around a town: they dance, they shop, they cheer the
band, they queue for pies.

The idea that shaped it was simple: what if a UK Christmas market turned up in medieval
Skyrim? The lanterns strung between the stalls, something warm to drink, the smell of
roasting meat, music drifting over the crowd, and far too many stalls selling far too many
things. Warm, handmade, a little chaotic, and busy in the best way.

So that's what I made: a travelling festival that has pitched its walls beside the road
west of Whiterun, and grown into a little village of its own. There's a stage with a live
band, a market of 33 stalls, an archery range, folk dancing on the green and fireworks over
the walls. And there's a crowd of visitors who actually seem to be having a good time.

I wanted it to feel like a real place that happens to be having a very good week, not a
theme park and not a parody. Most of all, I wanted somewhere you'd want to come back to:
after a hard day's dragon-slaying, or just because you fancy a sweetroll and a song.

Oh, and there's a horse on the stage roof. Nobody knows how it got up there. The Fair
Inspector is working on it.

## Installation instructions

1. Download with **Mod Organizer 2** or **Vortex** ("Mod Manager Download").
2. Enable `SkyrimFair.esp`. It only needs Skyrim itself, so it can go anywhere in your load order.
3. Head west out of Whiterun along the road towards the Western Watchtower. Look for the
   signpost, the banners and the braziers at the gate.

**Using a grass cache** (No Grass In Objects or Grass Cache Helper NG, as most big modlists
do)? The fair's ground has no grass until you regenerate your cache with the fair installed.
Nothing breaks without it, it's just less green.

**Using Maximum Destruction, Stealth Detection Fixes or Strange Runes?** Please make the
one-word edits in **Compatibility**, at the bottom of this page, or let the optional
**SPID Patcher** make them for you. Without them, the game freezes at the fair.

**Updating:** some updates change the stage show, which your save remembers. If an update
says so, visit from a new game, or from a save made before you first went to the fair.

## Main features

**A live stage show, with an original soundtrack**
- Six songs written for the fair, from "The Wanderer's Fair" itself to "Raise Your Cups".
- A band and an orchestra, with three singers out front who can't stop cheering.
- The crowd dances, claps and cheers along, and every song ends in fireworks.
- Folk dancers on the green, with a dance made just for them.
- With **Professional Dancer** installed, the crowd dances with even more moves.

**A market you can actually shop at**
- 33 stalls, each with its own trade and its own goods on display: fresh bread, roast meat,
  honey, cheese, mead, smoked fish, furs, pottery, toys, books, elven and Dwemer curios,
  Imperial and Stormcloak armour, a fortune teller, and a sweetroll stall doing suspiciously
  well.
- Every keeper runs a real shop. Chat to them, or just browse the counter.
- Festival prices: the armour and weapon stalls charge a premium (it's a fair, and the
  traders know it). Food, drink and everything else sell at normal prices.

**Made for the fair**
- Hand-made paper lanterns in twelve styles, glowing along every rope at night.
- Festival bunting in six colours: Whiterun, Saturalia, Riften, Windhelm, Imperial and
  Stormcloak.
- A timber palisade and gate, light towers, outhouses (it's a festival, after all), and the
  Fair Inspector's notices pinned around the walls.
- The fair's own gateway on the road, with braziers, banners and lanterns. You'll hear the
  music before you see it, and at night you'll see the fireworks over the walls.

**Life at the fair**
- A crowd of visitors: families with children, people sat taking it all in, and dancers who
  can't keep still.
- Archers practising at the range, goats and chickens in the pens, and dogs underfoot.
- Your companions are welcome too, and follow you in and out through the gate.

**Familiar faces**
- Two of the fair's visitors may seem oddly familiar. One is a cheerful bard who insists the
  fair is completely under control (please don't ask follow-up questions). The other carries
  a ledger, has counted the lanterns, and has concerns. Both are fully voiced, both wander
  the fair, and each has plenty to say. **Can you spot them?**

**The Mystery of the Horse on the Roof**
- One morning, a horse was found standing on the roof of the stage. It's perfectly calm.
  Nobody saw it arrive.
- The Fair Inspector has offered a reward of 500 gold and put up notices appealing for
  witnesses. So far, the only statement received was "I was drunk", which, the notices
  point out, is not considered a useful witness statement.
- Read the notices around the fair to follow the case. The horse is presently unharmed.
  The Inspector is not.

**Runs smoothly**
- Built to keep your frame rate up, even with a whole festival crowd around you.

## Requirements

**Required:** Skyrim Special Edition or Anniversary Edition. That's it.

**Recommended:**
- **Open Animation Replacer** (needs **SKSE**): the folk dance, the band's livelier
  playing and the singers' own cheer. Without it, they use Skyrim's own animations.
- **[Professional Dancer](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/124608)**
  (optional): more dance moves for the crowd. Enable `Dance.esp`, then run Pandora or
  Nemesis.
- **[Terrain Parallax](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/54860)**: extra
  depth on the fair's cobbles.

## Compatibility

### Using Stealth Detection Fixes, Maximum Destruction or Strange Runes? Read this

These mods give **every NPC** in the game a spell through SPID. The fair has a big crowd,
and with that many NPCs their spells flood the game's script engine. **About a minute after
you arrive, the game freezes:** the music stops and the performers stand still.

The fix is one word per line: `-SkyrimFairNPC`. It tells SPID to skip anyone at the fair
(every fair NPC carries the `SkyrimFairNPC` keyword). Nothing changes anywhere else in
your game.

**The easy way:** download the optional **SPID Patcher** file, and run
`skyrimfair_spid_patcher.py` (it needs [Python](https://www.python.org/downloads/) 3.8 or newer).
Point it at your MO2 `mods` folder, or at Skyrim's `Data` folder for Vortex. It makes the edits
below for you, keeps a backup of each file, and can put them back with `--undo`.

**By hand:** open each file below in a text editor (it's in the mod's folder; in Mod Organizer
2, right-click the mod and choose "Open in Explorer"). Find the line, and change it as
shown. Save the file. If you update the mod later, make the edit again.

#### Stealth Detection Fixes (three files)

`StealthKillDetectionFix_Attack_DISTR.ini`, change

```
Spell = 0x817~StealthKillDetectionFix.esp|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE
```

to

```
Spell = 0x817~StealthKillDetectionFix.esp|-SkyrimFairNPC|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE
```

`StealthKillDetectionFix_DISTR.ini`, change

```
Spell = 0x80B~StealthKillDetectionFix.esp|ActorTypeNPC|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE
```

to

```
Spell = 0x80B~StealthKillDetectionFix.esp|ActorTypeNPC,-SkyrimFairNPC|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE
```

`StealthKillDetectionFix_Killmove_DISTR.ini`, change

```
Spell = 0x819~StealthKillDetectionFix.esp|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE
```

to

```
Spell = 0x819~StealthKillDetectionFix.esp|-SkyrimFairNPC|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE
```

#### Maximum Destruction (one file)

`MaximumDestruction_DISTR.ini`, the line starting `Spell = 0x8E6289`. Add
`,-SkyrimFairNPC` after `Estormo`:

```
Spell = 0x8E6289~MaximumDestruction.esp|ActorTypeNPC,Charmed Vigilant,Spellsword,Arch-Curate Vyrthur,Estormo,-SkyrimFairNPC|NONE|NONE|NONE|NONE|100
```

#### Strange Runes (one file)

`StrangeRunes_DISTR.ini`, change

```
Spell = 0x68855~StrangeRunes.esp|ActorTypeNPC
```

to

```
Spell = 0x68855~StrangeRunes.esp|ActorTypeNPC,-SkyrimFairNPC
```

#### Other mods like these

Any mod whose SPID file gives *every* NPC a cloak or a script can do the same. Add
`-SkyrimFairNPC` to the second field of its `Spell =` line (after a comma if the field
already has something in it, or in place of `NONE`).

**Mod authors:** adding `-SkyrimFairNPC` to your own line is harmless for players without
the fair, and saves them the edit.
